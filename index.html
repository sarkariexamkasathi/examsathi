SSC CGL

Combined Graduate Level — Group B & C posts
Conducting Body
Staff Selection Commission
Mode
Online (CBT)
Tiers
2 (Tier I + Tier II)

Exam Pattern

1
Tier I — 100 Q · 200 Marks · 60 min
Qualifying. 4 sections (25 Q each): Reasoning, General Awareness, Quant, English. Negative marking: 0.50/wrong.
2
Tier II — Paper 1, 2 & 3
Paper 1 compulsory (Maths & Reasoning, English, GA + Computer). Paper 2 (Statistics) & Paper 3 sirf specific posts ke liye. Final merit yahin decide hoti hai.
3
Document Verification
Certificates check aur physical verification

Subject-wise Topics

Quantitative Aptitude

Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics

Reasoning

Analogy, Symbolic Operations, Coding-Decoding, Series, Puzzle

English Language

Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary, Error Spotting

General Awareness

History, Polity, Geography, Current Affairs, Science

Source: ssc.gov.in — official notification zaroor cross-check karo, pattern saal ke hisaab se revise hota hai.

Banking — IBPS PO

Probationary Officer — Public Sector Banks
Conducting Body
IBPS
Mode
Online (CBT)
Stages
3 (Prelims, Mains, Interview)

Exam Pattern

1
Prelims — 100 Q · 100 Marks · 60 min
English (30), Quant (35), Reasoning (35). Qualifying — marks final merit mein nahi jodte. Negative marking: 0.25/wrong.
2
Mains — 200 Objective + 25 Descriptive = 225 Marks
Reasoning & Computer, Data Analysis & Interpretation, GA/Banking, English, Letter & Essay Writing.
3
Interview
Final merit = 80% Mains + 20% Interview

Subject-wise Topics

Quantitative Aptitude

Simplification, Data Interpretation, Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic

Reasoning

Puzzle, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Inequality

English Language

Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Detection

General Awareness

Banking Awareness, Current Affairs, Static GK

Source: ibps.in — official notification zaroor cross-check karo.

Railway — RRB NTPC

Non-Technical Popular Categories — Station Master, Clerk, Goods Train Manager
Conducting Body
Railway Recruitment Board
Mode
Online (CBT)
Stages
2 CBT + Skill Test/CBAT

Exam Pattern

1
CBT 1 — 100 Q · 100 Marks · 90 min
General Awareness (40), Mathematics (30), Reasoning (30). Screening stage. Negative marking: 1/3 per wrong.
2
CBT 2 — 120 Q · 120 Marks · 90 min
Same subjects, higher difficulty: GA (50), Mathematics (35), Reasoning (35). Merit yahin decide hoti hai.
3
Skill Test / CBAT + DV
Typing test (clerk posts) ya CBAT (Station Master), phir document verification & medical.

Subject-wise Topics

Mathematics

Number System, Percentage, Time & Work, Mensuration, Geometry

Reasoning

Analogy, Classification, Coding-Decoding, Puzzle

General Awareness

Current Affairs, History, Geography, Polity, Science

Source: RRB official sites — zone ke hisaab se notification alag hoti hai, apne zone ka site check karo.

Teaching — CTET

Central Teacher Eligibility Test — Classes I–VIII
Conducting Body
CBSE
Mode
Offline (OMR)
Papers
2 (Paper I + Paper II)

Exam Pattern

1
Paper I — 150 Q · 150 Marks · 2.5 hrs
Classes I–V ke liye. Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, Maths, Environmental Studies (30 Q each). No negative marking.
2
Paper II — 150 Q · 150 Marks · 2.5 hrs
Classes VI–VIII ke liye. Child Development, Language I & II, aur Maths+Science YA Social Studies (choice). No negative marking.

Subject-wise Topics

Child Development & Pedagogy

Growth stages, learning theories, inclusive education

Language I & II

Grammar, comprehension, pedagogy of language teaching

Mathematics / Science

NCERT-based, pedagogy of teaching maths/science

Environmental / Social Studies

NCERT-based, teaching pedagogy

Score lifetime valid hai — sirf ek baar CTET pass karna hota hai. Source: ctet.nic.in

Defence — NDA

National Defence Academy — Army, Navy, Air Force
Conducting Body
UPSC
Mode
Offline (Written)
Stages
Written + SSB Interview

Exam Pattern

1
Paper I — Mathematics — 120 Q · 300 Marks · 2.5 hrs
+2.5 correct, -0.83 wrong. Class 10-12 level maths.
2
Paper II — GAT — 150 Q · 600 Marks · 2.5 hrs
Part A: English (200), Part B: General Knowledge — Physics, Chemistry, History, Geography, Current Events (400). +4 correct, -1.33 wrong.
3
SSB Interview — 900 Marks
4-5 din ka process: OIR, PP&DT, Interview, Psychology Tests, Group Tasks, Conference.

Subject-wise Topics

Mathematics

Algebra, Trigonometry, Calculus, Vectors, Statistics

English

Grammar, Comprehension, Vocabulary

General Knowledge

Physics, Chemistry, History, Geography, Current Events

Total selection: 1800 marks (Written 900 + SSB 900). Source: upsc.gov.in
